About me

I am an international affairs professional focused on one mission: making global opportunity accessible to talented people who deserve it. My work sits at the intersection of global partnerships, student & worker placement, and cross-cultural communication.

Born and raised in Aceh, Indonesia, I studied Development Economics at Universitas Samudra, earned a Romanian Government scholarship for my Master’s in Economics & International Business at Ovidius University, and spent an Erasmus+ exchange year at Universität Kassel in Germany. That journey taught me what talent mobility actually demands — not just paperwork, but trust on both sides of the border.

Today I help Indonesian students and workers build futures in Germany and across Europe — coordinating Ausbildung and employment placement at Bright Education Indonesia and as a recruitment partner for Medwing Germany, across agencies AuLiD, BAI, Talentle, BeRi and DEB. Since 2024 my pipelines have carried 268 candidates, with 143 already in Germany in 2026. I speak four languages, I organize international programs, and I write about the experience so others can follow.
